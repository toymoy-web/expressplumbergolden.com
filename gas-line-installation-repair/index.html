Gas line work in Golden covers new installations for a range, water heater, or outdoor fire feature, along with repair of an existing line showing a leak or pressure issue. Any suspected gas leak is treated as urgent, and we coordinate with the gas utility when a situation calls for it.

Local Factors

What We See Most in Golden

Homes converting from electric to gas appliances, especially during a water heater replacement, are one of the most common gas line calls we get, since it involves running a new line rather than working with an existing one. We size and route new lines to current code, accounting for Golden's altitude when it affects appliance combustion ratings.

On older homes, gas line repair calls sometimes trace back to a fitting that's corroded over decades or a line that was never properly sized for an appliance added years after original construction.

Signs to Watch For

Signs You Need Gas Line Service

Smell of gas anywhere near the line or an appliance

Leave the area, don't use switches or flames, and call immediately, including the gas utility's emergency line.

Appliance not reaching full performance

Can indicate a line that's undersized or has a partial restriction.

Visible corrosion on an older gas fitting

Worth inspecting before it becomes an active leak.

Planning a new gas appliance install

Range, water heater, or outdoor fire feature installs all need a properly sized and permitted line.

Prevention

Keeping Gas Lines Safe Between Inspections

A yearly visual check of any exposed gas fittings, especially on an older home, catches surface corrosion before it becomes a leak. Look for a greenish-white powder on brass fittings or a white chalky residue on black iron pipe, both early signs of corrosion worth having a licensed technician evaluate.

If you smell a faint gas odor only occasionally, don't dismiss it as your imagination. Intermittent smells, especially after weather changes or ground settling, can indicate a small leak that's only detectable under certain conditions, and it's worth a professional leak check rather than waiting for it to become constant.

FAQs

Common Questions

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the area immediately, avoid using any switches or open flames, and call the gas utility's emergency line as well as us.

Do you handle the permit for new gas line work?

Yes, permitting is included as part of any new gas line installation.

Can you add a gas line for an outdoor fire pit or grill?

Yes, we run and properly size outdoor gas lines and can coordinate shutoff valve placement. If it turns out to be related, our Commercial Plumbing page covers that separately.
