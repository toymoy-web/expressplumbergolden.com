Faucet and sink repair in Golden, CO is one of the most common calls we take, and it's rarely just a dripping annoyance. A faucet that won't fully shut off, a valve that's seized, or a sink with a slow leak under the cabinet all point to the same underlying issue in this market: hard mineral deposits wearing out internal cartridges and washers faster than they would in a softer-water city.

We repair and replace kitchen and bathroom faucets, shut-off valves, supply lines, and sink drains, and we'll tell you plainly when a $40 cartridge fixes it versus when the whole fixture is past the point of repair.

Local Factors

Why Faucets Wear Out Faster in Golden

Golden's municipal water, drawn from Clear Creek, carries enough mineral content that it deposits scale inside faucet cartridges and valve seats over time. That buildup is what causes a faucet to drip steadily even after the handle is fully closed, or a valve to feel stiff and eventually seize.

Older homes near downtown Golden with original fixtures see this most, since decades of hard water have had time to work through multiple cartridge generations. Newer fixtures with ceramic disc cartridges hold up somewhat better, but nothing is immune long-term.

Signs to Watch For

Common Faucet & Sink Problems We Fix

Steady drip that won't stop

Usually a worn cartridge or washer inside the faucet body, not a full fixture replacement.

Handle is hard to turn

Mineral buildup on the valve stem or cartridge causing resistance, sometimes with a squeak.

Water pooling under the sink

Often a loose or corroded supply line connection rather than the faucet itself.

Low pressure from one faucet only

A clogged aerator or partially scaled cartridge restricting flow at just that fixture.

Prevention

Making a New Faucet Last Longer in Golden's Water

Since scale buildup from hard water is the main reason faucets fail here, a simple monthly habit extends a new fixture's life meaningfully: unscrew the aerator at the tip of the spout and soak it in white vinegar for 15-20 minutes to dissolve mineral deposits before they restrict flow or seize the cartridge.

If you're installing a new faucet anyway, a ceramic disc cartridge holds up noticeably better against Golden's mineral content than an older rubber-washer design, and it's worth requesting specifically rather than accepting whatever the fixture ships with by default.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you fix a dripping faucet the same day?

Yes, most faucet repairs are same-day since we carry common cartridges and washers on the truck.

Should I replace or repair an older faucet?

If the body and finish are in good shape, repair is usually cheaper; if it's discontinued or the body itself is cracked, replacement makes more sense.

Why does my faucet feel stiff to turn?

That's typically mineral buildup on the valve stem from Golden's hard water, which we can often clear during a repair visit. If it turns out to be related, our Water Softener Installation & Repair page covers that separately.
